# Insights & News editorial library

The website page is src/pages/insights-news.astro, available at /insights-news. The approved design reference remains editorial/archive/insights-news-approved-preview.html.
It contains the decision-infrastructure and private-credit perspectives. The private-credit article incorporates BIS research alongside ACC/AIMA sources. The page is implemented locally; deployment is a separate step.

## Draft queue

1. drafts/03-professional-judgement.html — Scale the process. Preserve the judgement.
2. drafts/04-productive-growth.html — Help suitable capital reach productive businesses.

Each draft preserves the complete tile, quotation, sources, and article as HTML.
Edit these files here until ready. They are outside public and src/pages and are
not included in the static website build. They are not scheduled for automatic publication.

## Publishing a perspective

- Review the text, quotation and linked research.
- Add its card and article dialog to the Insights & News page in src/pages/insights-news.astro.
- Set its actual publication date when integrating the production page.
- Prepare one accompanying LinkedIn post linking to the published article.
- Preview, then deploy when authorised. Move the editorial draft to a published
  folder after release and record the live article URL.

The intended cadence is one perspective per week, with dates chosen at launch.
Company news and events can join the same collection when there is real news to share.
