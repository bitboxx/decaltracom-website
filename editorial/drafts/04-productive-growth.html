Help suitable capital reach productive businesses.

Better financial decisions have a wider purpose: enabling institutions to understand businesses and assess the finance they need to invest and grow.

The fit of finance matters

The Bank of England’s discussion of productive investment emphasises matching firms with appropriate finance while maintaining resilience. Its review of high-growth firms identifies information gaps and financing barriers, alongside the importance of equity for some innovation-led businesses.

Understanding before allocation

Our reading is that increasing the volume of lending is only one part of the question. Institutions need to understand the business, its stage of development, and the risks of the proposed funding.

Our ambition

DecAltra aims to make the work behind those decisions more efficient and traceable. By connecting information, analysis, and professional judgement, we want to help institutions spend more time understanding opportunities and less time preparing the same information repeatedly.
