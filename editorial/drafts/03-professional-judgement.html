Information & judgement03 / Perspective

Efficient credit assessment depends on more than processing financial figures. Business context, sector knowledge, and the ability to challenge assumptions remain central.

Main takeaways

  • Information processing and relationships both matter in lending.
  • Sector expertise can affect a business’s access to credit.
  • Our view: automation should help professionals apply expertise and review evidence.

DecAltra perspective · Information & judgement

Scale the process. Preserve the judgement.

Making credit assessment more efficient should give professionals more capacity to understand a business. That is the principle behind our approach to automation.

Information and relationships belong together

The ECB’s conceptual framework distinguishes information collection and processing from relationships and distribution. Both shape financial intermediation. Our interpretation is that improving the mechanics of analysis should go hand in hand with preserving the context professionals bring.

Understanding an industry matters

Banque de France research finds that credit declines following branch closures were greater when businesses moved to branches less specialised in their industry. This offers evidence that lender expertise matters alongside the availability of financial data.

Our response

DecAltra aims to connect source information, analysis, and professional judgement. We want analysts to spend less time reconstructing a case and more time testing assumptions, understanding sector conditions, and explaining their conclusions.

Automate preparation. Give judgement better foundations.
